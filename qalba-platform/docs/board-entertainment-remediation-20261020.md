# Board entertainment: first source-backed variety remediation, 2026-10-20

## Scope and limits

This author desk-research batch replaces **33 unpublished draft questions**: eight Movies400, eight Cars400, eight Series600, eight Games600, plus the previously easy Snow White clue in Movies600. Each replacement has a specific primary reference and a locator/evidence note in `content-review/2026-10-19/Board_Entertainment_7x96_Review.csv`. References were consulted live on 2026-10-20; the date in `notes` records this author lookup, **not** a human review.

All 672 entertainment candidates remain unpublished. Every category still has 96 items and every tier has 32. Runtime banks, seed activation, production data, and media are outside this batch. All existing human gates are preserved as `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_FACTCHECK`, `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_ITEM_REVIEW`, `NOT_RUN`, `NOT_RUN`; `reviewer` and `reviewed_at` remain blank. Alternative transliterations in the CSV are proposed answers only, not approved variants. The numeric tiers are provisional until blind player results exist.
